The answer is the easy part.

Anyone can write you a five day plan in thirty seconds. The work is checking any of it is still true, and then keeping hold of it for the six weeks before you fly.

What you get from a chat

A good looking plan, in a wall of text, in a thread you will not find again. So you open six tabs to check it yourself, and paste whatever survives into a note.

  • Room prices that were right last year

  • No record of what you have already booked

What you get from this

The same plan, with every number looked up as it was written, sitting in an app on your phone. Your days, your flights, your rooms, your booking references, and the short list of what is not sorted yet.

  • Room rates for your dates, in ringgit
  • Opening times looked up this week
  • Every booking filed with its reference
